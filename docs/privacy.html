MealBridge Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2 November 2026

Introduction

MealBridge respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

Information We Collect

MealBridge may process meal preferences, dietary requirements, shopping lists, meal history and recipe requests to provide its functionality.

MealBridge does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers or physical addresses unless voluntarily entered by the user.

AI Meal Generation

When you request AI-generated recipes or meal plans, your request may be securely transmitted to an AI service provider solely to generate the requested content.

Local Storage

MealBridge stores meal history, shopping lists and preferences locally on your device.

Personal Recipes and Optional Community Sharing

Recipes you create and favourite are saved on your device. Saving a recipe does not submit it to the community.

If you explicitly choose to submit a recipe, MealBridge sends the recipe name, ingredients, instructions, preparation and cooking times, servings and nutrition note to its cloud service. The service stores these recipe details with a submission receipt, consent version, consent timestamp and review status. Household profiles and pantry lists are not included in this submission.

Submitted recipes are held for review and are not automatically published. Do not include names or other personal information in recipe text you choose to share.

Data Sharing

MealBridge does not sell or rent your personal information.

Security

All communications with cloud services use encrypted HTTPS connections.

Data Deletion

You may remove locally stored information at any time by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application. Removing a favourite removes it from your favourites list; it does not delete a personal recipe or a recipe already submitted to the cloud.

Clearing or uninstalling the app does not delete a previously submitted community recipe. To request removal of a submitted recipe, contact support using the address below and identify the recipe.
